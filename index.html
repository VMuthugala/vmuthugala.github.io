02 / RESEARCH

What I work on

Most of my work starts from a real deployment: cleaning, inspection and maintenance robots that have to run in food courts, ship yards, drains, pavements and hospitals. Select a paper to see its details.

Reconfigurable robotics

Tiling, size-variable and inter-reconfigurable modular robots that change morphology to fit obstacles, cover more area and dock with each other.

Coverage & path planning

Complete coverage that accounts for energy, risk and shape: bio-inspired neural networks, reinforcement learning, metaheuristics and multi-agent scheduling.

Foundation models for autonomy

Vision-language and foundation models for robot task planning, the safety of VLM-controlled robots against physical prompt injection, and human–robot co-design of the spaces robots work in.

Human–robot interaction

Robots that interpret uncertain spatial language, read gestures and approach people appropriately. My PhD used neuro-fuzzy methods for navigational commands.

Robot-inclusive design

Auditing and designing buildings so robots can work in them, from lighting and floor unevenness to false ceilings, drains and tactile markers.

Service & field robots

Design and control of robots for ship-hull blasting, facade and wall cleaning, aircraft inspection, drain and ceiling inspection, and outdoor sweeping.
